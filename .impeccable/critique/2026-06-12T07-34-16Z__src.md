## Anti-Patterns Verdict

**LLM assessment:** FAIL. Two DESIGN.md violations are present in production code: (1) side-stripe border on testimonial cards (`Testimonios.module.css:32`) — a direct violation of the "Don't put colored side-stripe borders" rule, and (2) numbered step markers in checkout (`Checkout.tsx:182,268,295`) — violating "Don't saturate the page with numbered sections." These are the precise anti-patterns the design system was written to forbid, meaning the spec exists on paper but hasn't been enforced in code.

**Deterministic scan:** 1 finding confirmed — the `side-tab` border-left on testimonial cards. File: `src/pages/Testimonios/Testimonios.module.css:32`. The detector confirms exactly what the LLM review found.

**Visual overlays:** No browser automation available. No reliable user-visible overlay was produced. The findings are source-code based and reproducible by inspecting the rendered page manually.

## Overall Impression

The app has the bones of a genuinely warm, local e-commerce experience. The salmon-on-cream palette is a standout — it feels like a bakery, not a template. The cart drawer and product grid are well-executed. But two DESIGN.md violations reveal the spec exists but isn't enforced. The checkout stumbles hardest: email gate friction, alert() errors, discount non-feedback — all contradict the "flujo de compra sin fricción" principle. Biggest opportunity: delete the email gate, fix the two violations, add real payment-error recovery.

## What's Working

1. **Color system** is warm, cohesive, and brand-right — cream surface (#f9f6f0), salmon (#e57373), deep navy (#1a1a2e). The salmon appears sparingly and meaningfully.
2. **Cart drawer** is a UX bright spot — slides in from right, item thumbnails, inline quantity controls, helpful empty state with friendly copy.
3. **Mobile responsiveness** is solid — sidebar collapses to overlay, grids reflow to single column, consistent 768px breakpoints across all pages.

## Priority Issues

- **[P0] Side-stripe border on testimonial cards.** `Testimonios.module.css:32` — `border-left: 4px solid #e57373`. Direct DESIGN.md violation. **Fix:** Remove the side border; use a tinted background or nothing. → `/impeccable quieter Testimonios`

- **[P0] Numbered step markers in checkout.** `Checkout.tsx:182,268,295` — stepNum circles 1, 2, 3. DESIGN.md forbids numbered sections as default scaffolding. **Fix:** Remove step numbers, keep plain section titles. → `/impeccable quieter Checkout`

- **[P1] Payment errors use native alert().** `Checkout.tsx:108` — `alert('Error al procesar el pago')`. Jarring, breaks UX flow, no actionable info. **Fix:** Replace with inline error banner + retry button. → `/impeccable harden Checkout`

- **[P1] Discount code has no feedback.** `Checkout.tsx:151-159` — user clicks "Aplicar" and sees nothing. Zero visibility of system status. **Fix:** Add discount state (applied/rejected) with visual feedback. → `/impeccable harden Checkout`

- **[P2] Email-first checkout gate is confusing.** `Checkout.tsx:186-207` — enter email → "¿Has comprado anteriormente? Iniciar sesión" — a local bakery client doesn't expect an email signup to place an order. **Fix:** Remove email gate or replace with phone field; move account creation after order. → `/impeccable distill Checkout`

## Persona Red Flags

**Alex (Power User):** No saved delivery addresses, no order history, no wishlist, no reorder. Must enter billing info every time. No keyboard shortcuts beyond Escape.

**Jordan (First-Timer):** Email-first checkout is a barrier for a customer who walks into the physical shop. "~50 min" is abstract. Billing tabs ("boleta", "factura", "sinDatos") require Peruvian tax knowledge. No tooltips or "how it works" section.

**Casey (Mobile User):** Sidebar slide animation (0.3s) feels slow on mobile. "Contáctanos" button is hidden at mobile (Header.module.css:122-124). Product grid at 160px min may be too small on <375px phones.

## Minor Observations

- CartDrawer footer says "Pagar con Mercado Pago" but checkout also offers "Paga en el local" and "Pagar en efectivo" — mismatch implies MP is the only option from cart.
- Checkout time-tag uses emoji (🕐) via CSS `::before` while every other icon uses inline SVG — inconsistent.
- DeliveryMap loads marker from unpkg CDN — no offline fallback.
- No `<title>` updates per page — browser tab always shows app name only.
- Footer has no address, phone, social links, or hours — that info lives only on Contacto page.

## Questions to Consider

1. Is the email-first checkout solving a real business need or was it added because "that's how e-commerce works"? A phone-number-first flow would feel more natural for a traditional bakery in Ica.
2. The numbered steps (1, 2, 3) imply a linear process, but billing (step 3) comes after payment (step 2). Should the user choose payment method before knowing their total? Why not reorder: contact/delivery → billing → payment?
3. The Home hero says "Más de 80 años" proudly, yet the only history content is a single section below. Could the homepage tell the VELAZCO story through product imagery rather than text paragraphs?
